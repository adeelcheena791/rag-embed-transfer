## 4. New analyses and what they show [N]

**Query-level transitions (NQ, 10 encoder–reader pairs).** 14–19% of queries moved from miss to hit; within them EM rose by 0.26–0.34, from a MiniLM-L6 level of 0.23–0.30 (many of these "misses" were already answered). This group contributed +0.040 to +0.055 to ΔEM. Queries that hit under both retrievers (59–62%) contributed −0.021 to −0.007 (interval below zero in 7 of 10), queries that lost their hit (4–7%) −0.019 to −0.006 (10 of 10 below zero), and double misses ≈ 0 (9 of 10 intervals contain zero). The net gain was 29–72% of the gain from new hits alone. Contributions add up to ΔEM exactly (asserted).

**HotpotQA by supporting passages.** Queries for which the encoder retrieved more supporting passages (34–48%) carried 100–130% of the EM gain (all intervals above zero), mostly 1 → 2 passages (62–81%); same-count and fewer-count queries contributed small losses.

**Decomposition components.** NQ: prevalence +0.021 to +0.051; EM among hits −0.024 to −0.009 (below zero in 2 of 10); EM among misses −0.004 to +0.007 (contains zero in 10 of 10). HotpotQA: EM among hits +0.031 to +0.093 (all above zero); misses −0.003 to +0.000.

**Sensitivity.** NQ dense ratios at k = 5: EM 0.16–0.27, F1 0.23–0.33, containment 0.27–0.39, BM25 baseline 0.29–0.35, largest upper bound 0.60. By k (Qwen, exploratory): 0.41–0.83 at k = 1 (upper bound up to 1.22), 0.32–0.37 at k = 3, 0.05–0.19 at k = 10. HotpotQA: 0.49–0.93 with Hit@5, 0.29–0.35 with Both@5. Robust: on NQ, at k ≥ 3, every ratio and upper bound is well below 1. Sensitive: the exact size (baseline, metric, k) and the HotpotQA contrast (retrieval metric).

**Interpretation limits.** Transition groups are defined by retrieval outcomes; they locate the EM difference, not its cause. Decomposition shares are accounting identities.

## 9. Remaining limitations and unresolved issues

1. **Full-corpus answers (main validity gap).** The full-corpus check covers retrieval order only. The full-corpus ranked lists were not stored, so no end-to-end comparison is possible from existing artifacts. Needs new GPU work (§10).
2. **NQ label incompleteness not measured.** Passage texts are not in the repository, and Hugging Face downloads are blocked from this workspace (HTTP 403), so no answer-string check or blinded audit of misses could be run here. Feasible on Kaggle CPU or the author's computer: extract the top-5 texts for NQ misses, run an automatic answer-alias containment check, and have the author (not an AI) label a seeded sample of about 100 misses for (a) factual correctness of the answer, (b) support in the retrieved passages, (c) answer-bearing but unlabelled passage, (d) scoring/alias error. An AI pre-screen may be used but must be reported as such, not as independent annotation.
3. HNSW answer quality not measured; E12 hard negatives may contain answers (O8).
4. Post hoc analyses are numerous; each is labelled and reported regardless of direction (two weakened the original HotpotQA headline).
5. Author-side items unchanged: repository visibility (O1), title-page facts (O5), AI-use declaration wording (O6).

## 10. Approval request: one focused GPU experiment

**Question.** Does the NQ transfer ratio of a strong dense encoder over MiniLM-L6 hold when retrieval runs over the full 2.68 M-passage corpus instead of the 300,000-passage sub-corpus?

**Design (smallest informative).** NQ, 2,255 generation queries (all, for precision), Qwen2.5-7B-AWQ reader, prompt A1, k = 5, greedy — identical to the Qwen–NQ cell. Retrievers: MiniLM-L6 (baseline) and e5-base-v2 (largest Hit@5 gain), exact flat search over the full corpus, same revisions and prefixes. Optional third retriever: BM25 on CPU (no GPU cost), which would test the sub-corpus bias directly. Save the full-corpus top-100 lists this time.

**Pre-specified analysis (to be written into a dated addendum v0.4 before the run).** T_full(e5-base-v2) with the existing reporting rule and bootstrap; the paired difference T_full − T_sub on the same queries with a bootstrap interval; criterion: T_full upper bound < 1, and T_full inside the sub-corpus interval [0.15, 0.36]. Report EM, Hit@5 and the transition table for both corpora.

**What it could change.** If T_full stays in [0.15, 0.36], the main NQ claim holds beyond the sub-corpus. If it rises markedly (e.g. because many full-corpus "misses" become answer-bearing unlabelled hits), the abstract and conclusion would need to say that the low ratio is partly a sub-corpus property.

**Workload.** Encoding 2,681,468 passages at the measured T4 throughput: MiniLM-L6 ≈ 0.41 h, e5-base-v2 ≈ 1.51 h; search negligible; reader 2 × ≈ 0.4 h (Phase 2 timing: 1,410 s per 2,255 queries at k = 5). Total ≈ 2.7–3.0 T4-hours on the free Kaggle quota, $0. Memory: a 768-d float32 index of 2.68 M passages ≈ 8.2 GB, within the 31 GiB session.

**Requested decision.** Approve this run (and whether to include BM25), and confirm that you will write and commit the dated addendum before it starts. I have not started it.
