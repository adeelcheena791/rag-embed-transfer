\section*{Data and code availability}
All code, prompts, design documents with their registration dates, raw reader outputs, scored answers and analysis scripts are kept in one version-controlled repository. To preserve double-anonymised review, its link is given to the editor on the separate title page; it will be made public, with an archived DOI, on acceptance. The datasets and models are public under their own licences; the exact revisions are listed in Appendix~\ref{app:repro}. Every number in this paper is generated from the committed analysis output.

During the preparation of this work the author used Claude (Anthropic) to write and test analysis and experiment code, to check references against bibliographic databases, and to draft and edit text. After using this tool, the author reviewed and edited the content as needed and takes full responsibility for the content of the published article. Figures and tables were generated by scripts from the experimental data.
