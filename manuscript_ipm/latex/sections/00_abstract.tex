\begin{abstract}
Teams that build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems often pick an embedding model by its retrieval score. We measured how much of a retrieval gain reaches the answer, and at what cost, for open models on free cloud GPUs. We compared BM25 and six open embedding models (\ev{E7.par.R1}--\ev{E7.par.R6}M parameters) on Natural Questions (NQ, \ev{N.nq.joined} queries) and HotpotQA (\ev{N.hp.gen} queries), each read by two quantised 7--8B models. The transfer ratio $T$ divides the exact-match (EM) gain by the Hit@5 gain over a small baseline encoder on the same queries. We report bootstrap intervals, and we registered six predictions before the second phase of the study. On NQ, $T$ ranged from \ev{T.lnq.R4} to \ev{T.lnq.R3}, and no upper 95\% bound exceeded \ev{T.qnq.R6.hi}. On HotpotQA, $T$ was larger (\ev{T.qhp.R6}--\ev{T.lhp.R4}) and one upper bound reached \ev{T.lhp.R4.hi}, so the prediction $T<1$ failed in one of four reader--dataset cells. An exact decomposition attributes \ev{DS.hp.min}--\ev{DS.hp.max} of the HotpotQA transfer to higher EM given a hit, in line with retrieving both supporting passages. On HotpotQA, bge-large gave higher EM than e5-base-v2 (\ev{P2.qhp.R4} and \ev{P2.lhp.R4}, Holm-adjusted $p \le$ \ev{P2.lhp.R4.p}); on NQ, no difference was detected. Encoding throughput on one T4 GPU differed \ev{E7.ratio.R1R6}-fold across encoders. Top-ranked non-gold passages lowered EM by \ev{P5.abs} against random passages; gold position showed no detectable effect. An LLM faithfulness judge agreed poorly with human labels (Cohen's $\kappa$ = \ev{JA.kappa}) and was excluded. Within this setup, retrieval gains overstate answer gains on single-hop questions, and encoder cost should be weighed alongside retrieval scores.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item On NQ, at most \ev{T.lnq.R3} EM points per Hit@5 point reached the answer
\item On multi-hop HotpotQA, transfer was larger and its intervals reached 1
\item Higher EM given a hit carries \ev{DS.hp.min}--\ev{DS.hp.max} of the HotpotQA transfer
\item Encoding throughput on a free T4 GPU differed \ev{E7.ratio.R1R6}-fold across six encoders
\item Three of six registered predictions failed in at least one condition
\end{highlights}
